# Reports

Candidates follow the official type and candidate-number order. A green **No
report** label means that this project has not published an attack report for
the candidate; it is not a security assessment or a claim that the candidate
is secure. Severity is the report's overall rating. Click an issue for the full
report, including reproduction steps. **Design** means the finding follows from
the submitted construction or specification; **implementation** means it is in
the submitted code, API, or integration rather than the specified construction.
